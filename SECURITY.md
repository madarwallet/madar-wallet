# Security policy

Madar Wallet is a self-custody wallet: your recovery phrase and keys are created and kept on your device. We take reports
about anything that could put users' funds or privacy at risk seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

- Email **contact@madarwallet.app** with the subject **"Security"**.
- Describe the problem, the steps to reproduce it and the impact you expect.
- Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, and do not test against other people's funds or accounts.
- Give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you share details publicly.

We reply as fast as we can, keep you informed while we fix it, and credit reporters who wish to be named.

## Scope

- The wallet code in this repository (browser extension and Android app).
- The websites madarwallet.app and swap.madarwallet.app.
